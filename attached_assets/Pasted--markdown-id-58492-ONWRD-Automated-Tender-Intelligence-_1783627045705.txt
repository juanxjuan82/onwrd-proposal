# ONWRD Automated Tender Intelligence Engine — Bahamas-First Scoring & Discovery Refinement

## Objective

Refine the existing Automated Tender Intelligence Engine so it operates as a Bahamas-first business development intelligence system for ONWRD.

ONWRD is a Bahamas-based full-service marketing and communications agency specializing in:

- Marketing campaigns
- Branding
- Communications strategy
- Digital marketing
- Social media
- Tourism promotion
- Community engagement
- Public awareness campaigns
- Stakeholder engagement
- Creative production
- Development-sector communications

The goal is NOT to maximize the number of tenders discovered.

The goal is:

> Identify the highest-probability opportunities ONWRD can realistically win based on geography, sector alignment, buyer type, relationships, and capability fit.

Keep the existing architecture:
- source adapters
- crawler system
- AI scoring pipeline
- keyword fallback scoring
- PURSUE / CONSIDER / SKIP workflow

Do not rebuild the system.

The system is successful when:

1. ONWRD receives a small number of highly relevant opportunities daily.

2. Bahamas opportunities naturally rank highest.

3. Caribbean opportunities are prioritized over generic global opportunities.

4. The AI avoids irrelevant procurement categories.

5. Every opportunity explains:
   - why it fits
   - why ONWRD has an advantage
   - why it may not be worth pursuing.

6. The system behaves like a Bahamas-based business development director, not a generic tender scraper.
