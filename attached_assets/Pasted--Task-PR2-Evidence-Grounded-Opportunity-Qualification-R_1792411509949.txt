## Task: PR2 — Evidence-Grounded Opportunity Qualification

**Repository:** `juanxjuan82/onwrd-proposal`

**Prerequisite:** PR #2 has passed review and been merged.

### Objective

Improve the qualification of procurement opportunities against ONWRD Advisors' actual capabilities, without increasing irrelevant recommendations or unnecessary AI expenditure.

Build on the existing scoring, eligibility and search-profile architecture. Do not replace functioning modules without justification.

### Requirements

**1. Separate evidence completeness from opportunity relevance.**

Implement explicit classifications for:

- `needs_enrichment` — insufficient source evidence
- `qualified` — relevant, with sufficient supporting evidence
- `not_qualified` — demonstrably irrelevant or ineligible
- `needs_human_review` — uncertain fit or eligibility

Do not permanently reject an opportunity because the crawler has not retrieved its complete scope.

Preserve compatibility with existing database states and proposal workflows.

**2. Improve ONWRD capability matching.**

Evaluate opportunities across:

- Strategic communications and public relations
- Branding and creative campaigns
- Digital marketing and content
- Tourism and destination marketing
- Stakeholder and community engagement
- Research, public awareness and development communications
- Strategic advisory and consulting
- Digital transformation, web platforms and automation

Use the existing ONWRD knowledge base and credentials where available. Do not infer qualifications that the company cannot substantiate.

**3. Introduce bounded AI qualification.**

Preserve inexpensive deterministic filtering for obvious exclusions, followed by semantic evaluation of plausible opportunities with sufficient source evidence.

Implement:

- Configurable AI evaluation budget
- Existing quota and circuit-breaker integration
- Deterministic fallback
- Structured machine-readable output
- Model and prompt version tracking
- No fabricated eligibility claims
- Evidence citations or supporting excerpts

**4. Produce actionable bid/no-bid assessments.**

For each qualified opportunity, return:

- Fit score and explanation
- Relevant ONWRD credentials
- Eligibility and mandatory requirements
- Missing qualifications
- Potential partnership requirements
- Geography and delivery constraints
- Submission deadline and preparation feasibility
- Bid recommendation with confidence level

A high relevance score must never override a confirmed hard eligibility failure.

**5. Make search profiles functional.**

Audit the existing `tender_search_profiles` configuration.

Ensure enabled profiles influence qualification as intended, or clearly document and correct any disconnected functionality.

**6. Preserve human decisions.**

Previously reviewed, rejected or accepted opportunities must not have their human decisions overwritten by automated rescoring.

### Acceptance criteria

- Relevant opportunities with unfamiliar terminology are correctly recognized.
- Generic procurement notices are not automatically promoted.
- Incomplete records are flagged for enrichment, not misclassified as irrelevant.
- AI results are grounded in actual procurement documentation.
- Scoring is reproducible and auditable.
- AI usage remains bounded.
- Existing proposal-generation workflows continue working.
- Regression tests cover false positives, false negatives, uncertain cases and eligibility failures.

### Delivery

Open a separate **draft PR2** with implementation details, tests and representative before/after qualification results.

Run typecheck and relevant tests, request code review, and do not merge or deploy without authorization.
