## Where conflicts could occur

The main risk is `artifacts/api-server/src/crawlers/index.ts`, which currently handles discovery, scoring, promotion and run tracking in one file.

I would establish these boundaries:

- PR1 owns: `crawlers/` source adapters, notice extraction and discovery refresh.
- PR2 owns: qualification services, scoring logic, eligibility rules and their tests.
- PR3 owns: telemetry services, diagnostics, source-health APIs and dashboard components.

PR2 and PR3 should avoid changing `crawlers/index.ts` until integration. Instead, implement their functionality in separate modules with clear interfaces.

That allows meaningful parallel development without having three agents repeatedly rebasing overlapping changes.
