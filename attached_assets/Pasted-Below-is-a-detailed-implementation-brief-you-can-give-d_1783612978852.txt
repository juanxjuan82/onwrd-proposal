# ONWRD Proposal Desk 2.0 — AI Bid Intelligence & Proposal Automation Platform

## Product Goal

Transform ONWRD Proposal Desk from an AI proposal generator into an automated bid intelligence platform optimized for:

- Bahamas government opportunities
- Statutory agencies
- NGOs
- UN agencies
- IDB and multilateral development opportunities
- Caribbean development-sector bids

Primary objective:

> Reduce the time from RFP discovery to submission-ready proposal while improving bid selection accuracy and proposal quality.

The system should automatically:
1. Discover opportunities
2. Extract requirements
3. Score fit and win probability
4. Build a proposal strategy
5. Generate a grounded proposal draft
6. Critique and improve the draft
7. Track pipeline performance and outcomes

Human users should focus only on:
- pursue/no-bid decisions
- providing missing information
- final approval

---
